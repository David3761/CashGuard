## Decisions taken on the way (to confirm)

1. **A signature counts only with a category, merchant or item match.** Without this rule, an unrelated signature with no range and no window scored 0.25 · strength · confidence from the amount and time terms alone.
2. **The amount term below the range** mirrors the rule above it: it reaches 0 at half of `amount_min`.
3. **Rule matching** (C8 owns it; this is a minimal copy in `life_fit.py`):
   - A rule applies up to **2× `amount_max`** (5.3.7).
   - The window is `from ≤ t < to`, in Bucharest time.
4. **Recurring bill:**
   - Only in the bill categories: rent, utilities, telecom, subscriptions, sport, education, kids, transfers and other. Otherwise Matei's Shop&Go 350 on the 15th would match his 350 utilities bill.
   - The match is a fixed cost within ±10% of the amount and ±3 days of its due day.
5. **Pot match:** an active `event` pot whose planned life event's type maps to the transaction's category (trip → travel, gift → gifts, social → restaurants / entertainment, education → education / books), on or before the pot's deadline.
6. **No goal** → `goal_impact` 0. The C7 tie-break then decides: the older transaction first, then the lower id.
7. **Weekly cap:** counted per ISO week.
8. **Back-off:** starts when the last 2 asked questions were both ignored (expired, or unanswered for 24 h). It lasts 7 days from the second one, and allows 1 question every 2 days.
9. **A suppress rule** turns `ask` into `auto` in C6, and C7 also skips a candidate whose transaction matches one.
10. **D50, kids prior:** with 0 children there's no kids prior. F2's `Baseline` needs a multiplier > 0, so the "× 0" from D50 can't be stored.
11. **Missing baseline:** when the context has no baseline for a category, C6 computes it from `data/priors.json` with E6's code.
