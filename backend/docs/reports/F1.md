### F1.2: Settings + `.env.example`

> Plan: [`docs/plans/F1.md`](../plans/F1.md) §F1.2, approved by David G. on 2026-10-29 with Q1 = fallback **off**, Q3 = conftest fixtures **yes**, Q4 = keep `KEYCLOAK_CRON_CLIENT_SECRET`.

**What & why.** Every environment variable the app reads, typed and validated in one frozen `Settings` model, so a bad value stops the app at startup instead of breaking the demo later. A1 is blocked on it. `create_app(settings)` now stores the settings on the app; routes read them through a typed dependency. Tests are hermetic: they never read the developer's real `.env` or shell variables, where a real key would mean real, paid Claude calls.

**Contract impact.**
- 🆕 `app.config`: `Settings`, `get_settings()` (cached), `get_app_settings(request)` (FastAPI dependency), `BACKEND_DIR`, `ENV_FILE`.
